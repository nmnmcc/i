1. Representation cannot change source acceptance, principal inference, library treatment or ABI. Numeric representation facts must be post-inference facts, never unsound displayed/known types. Unknown cases fall back, never reject otherwise legal i.
2. Preserve exact binary64 rounding at each source operation, signed zero through inspect/bits/ABI, division by either zero returning positive zero, no NaN/Infinity as i values, stop at first nonfinite intermediate even if dead. No reassociation/FMA or equality-based signed-zero substitution. Preserve source evaluation order and conditional evaluation.
3. Preserve arbitrary Int, signed division/remainder, zero-divisor convention, min/-1 and all overflow/shift/conversion edges. A failed guard must continue from original operands without repeated effects or lost intermediates.
4. Preserve equality/hash/type tests, identity of closures even under cloning/specialization, contents and order of aggregates. Scalar replacement cannot duplicate closure construction or skip eager unused fields.
5. Preserve exact source stop traces and tail positions across inlining, numeric clones, errors, cancellation and lazy pending frames. Different physical stack/heap exhaustion points and timing are allowed; source frames are not optional at O2.
6. Every scalar live across a potentially suspending call must have typed capture/re-entry storage or boxed fallback. This includes numeric call results, live operand temps, joins, recursive calls, ensure finals on cancellation, resumed code which suspends again, host handoff, and dynamic service calls with no suspension effect in their types.
7. Task go snapshots complete immutable captures/kept environment; box before generic capture/serialization or define bit-preserving typed codec. Preserve function identity and portability/pinning. Never optimize away retained environment frames (runtime §6.3 / lazy D16 workaround).
8. Numeric rewrites should apply equally to unrelated geometry, codecs/bit manipulation, finance/statistics and user math, with no std/float recognition.
9. Proofs need conservative binary64 rounding-aware ranges and explicit joins/loops/widening; unknown means retain check. Float finite operands alone do not imply finite result. Underflow and negative zero remain observable.
