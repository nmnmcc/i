Review criteria recorded before reading the change:
1. Soundness: every accepted program satisfies the typing rules and cannot go wrong from a type mismatch or missing service. Execute suspect accepted candidates at --opt 0 and --opt 2.
2. Principality: each inferred binding scheme must represent every valid typing. Adding an annotation expressing a valid instance must preserve acceptance. Simplifying solver bounds must preserve their solution set, including parameter relationships across generalization and instantiation.
3. Termination: finite source must not make constraint propagation or bound normalization diverge; stress deep helper chains and wide unions and compare time where reliable.
4. Recursive types: genuine structural cycles through a variable must still produce E0505, including indirect cycles and cycles through invariant constructors. Reflexive or redundant row constraints with finite solutions must not be mistaken for structural cycles.
5. Service rows: service arguments compare by equality regardless of declared variance (4.5.4). One environment cannot contain the same service constructor at unequal arguments (9.2), including rows assembled indirectly or inside closures. Environment statements must subtract the supplied service while checking its arguments and preserve outside requirements of the supplied expression.
6. Declaration order: top-level dependency groups are inferred and generalized together; reordering declarations must preserve acceptance and equivalent inferred schemes.
7. Normalization must remain solution-preserving under all variances, several parameters, variable-plus-constructor rows, mutually recursive functions, and local environment statements. The ordinary value restriction and captured nongeneralized variable relationships must remain intact.
